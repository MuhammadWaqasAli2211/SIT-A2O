# Arabic-script shaping for the onboarding-form export PDFs (three of the
# four are bilingual Urdu, and the Half Nama is almost entirely Urdu prose).
# reportlab has no OpenType shaping engine of its own: it maps one codepoint
# to one glyph and nothing more, so Urdu text needs its joined letterforms
# (arabic-reshaper) and its right-to-left reading order (python-bidi)
# resolved before reportlab ever sees it. Both are pure Python, no native
# dependencies - the same standard every other choice here is held to. See
# app/services/urdu_text.py for why the on-screen Nastaliq font could not be
# reused and what was substituted instead.

python-bidi==0.6.6
